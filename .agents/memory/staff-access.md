---
name: Staff access
description: Staff account registration and role assignment must remain invitation-only.
---

Staff and administrator accounts are created through invitations from an authorized administrator. Keep the staff sign-up route ticket-gated and assign the role from server-controlled invitation metadata; never let public sign-up choose a staff role.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected invitation-only staff registration.

**How to apply:** Preserve the invitation requirement whenever changing staff sign-up, account creation, or role assignment.
