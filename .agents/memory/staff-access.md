---
name: Staff access
description: Staff account registration and role assignment must remain invitation-only.
---

The two specifically authorized email accounts may self-register as Admins through the staff sign-up route, but only after server-side allowlist and verified-email checks. Other staff accounts remain invitation-only, with roles assigned from server-controlled invitation metadata. Never treat a hidden URL as authorization.

**Why:** The user selected allowlisted direct sign-up for two authorized addresses while retaining invitation-only access for other staff.

**How to apply:** Keep the allowlist on the server, require verified primary email before assigning Admin, and keep all other staff role assignment invitation-only.
