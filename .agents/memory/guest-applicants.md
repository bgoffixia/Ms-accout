---
name: Guest applicant path
description: Product requirements for account-free enrollment, evidence collection, and email verification.
---

Applicants can complete all enrollment details, the category PIN, and required private identity evidence/video without creating an account. The first screen collects only full name and email, then continues to a public/shared category-selection dashboard used by guest and signed-in users. Choosing a category starts the saved application and goes directly to category entry and PIN; do not insert a category-confirmation screen or a separate applicant dashboard into the new-application flow. Retired account-based applicant URLs should redirect to the shared dashboard, but do not delete their records or change admin review access. Clerk email verification of that same contact email is required for final submission and account linking; failure or postponement must leave the saved draft and evidence available to authorized reviewers.

Within one active applicant session, passing contact details must not send applicants back to that form through browser navigation or repeated start links. They may choose to return to the portal home, and reloading a public guest page should land on the portal home.

Applicants may advance through enrollment steps with incomplete fields. After Clerk verification, partial applications may be submitted if the category PIN is present; missing details, evidence, or declaration must remain visible as outstanding requirements. Save every step and correction for authorized reviewers, retaining prior entries so they can compare changes. Verified applicants should see their status, verification progress, and applicant-facing reviewer follow-up, while internal notes remain private. Never include raw PINs in reviewer history or store them in plaintext.

**Why:** The user asked applicants to complete enrollment without losing work to failed or postponed OTP, permit incomplete submission after verification while requiring the PIN, and let reviewers see corrections and verified applicants track progress. On October 13, 2026, the user specified one public shared category dashboard, direct navigation to category entry/PIN without a confirmation page, retiring old applicant links while keeping records for reviewers, skipping contact details for the remainder of an active session while allowing an explicit Home return and home on reload, and showing configured category and field copy in the applicant preview.

**How to apply:** Keep `/dashboard` public and shared across authentication states. After collecting contact details, category selection should create/save the guest draft and open category entry/PIN directly. Keep the contact step skipped within the active session, but always provide an explicit path to the portal home and return public guest-page reloads to home. In the final applicant preview, show the admin-configured category title, category-entry labels, workflow titles, and field labels; keep configured category-entry fields visible even when unanswered. Redirect applicant-only legacy routes to `/dashboard` without deleting their database records; keep admin review paths and role checks intact. Save each guest step and private evidence server-side before and during Clerk verification. Link only a verified primary email matching the contact email; keep drafts reviewer-visible if verification fails. Protect resume access with the private tracking key, preserve safe prior values for reviewer comparison, and keep internal reviewer notes private. Require the category PIN for submission without requiring other application fields, evidence, or declaration to be complete. Never store raw PINs. Do not add category, phone, or evidence fields to the first contact screen.
