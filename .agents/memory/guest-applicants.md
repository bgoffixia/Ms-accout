---
name: Guest applicant path
description: Product requirements for account-free enrollment, evidence collection, and email verification.
---

Applicants can complete all enrollment details, the category PIN, and required private identity evidence/video without creating an account. The first screen collects only full name and email, then continues to a public/shared category-selection dashboard used by guest and signed-in users. Choosing a category starts the saved application and goes directly to category entry and PIN; do not insert a category-confirmation screen or a separate applicant dashboard into the new-application flow. Retired account-based applicant URLs should redirect to the shared dashboard, but do not delete their records or change admin review access. Submission happens before email verification; clicking Submit opens the verification dialog, and the one-time code is sent after submission succeeds. Verifying the same contact email afterward links the application to the applicant account and redirects to the status page. Unverified submitted applications and evidence stay available to authorized reviewers.

Within one active applicant session, passing contact details must not send applicants back to that form through browser navigation or repeated start links. They may choose to return to the portal home, and reloading a public guest page should land on the portal home.

Applicants may advance through enrollment steps with incomplete fields. Submission requires the category PIN and applicant declaration, but does not wait for email verification; other incomplete details or evidence remain visible to reviewers. Save every step and correction for authorized reviewers, retaining prior entries so they can compare changes. Verified applicants should see their status, verification progress, and applicant-facing reviewer follow-up, while internal notes remain private. Keep the PIN hash for checks and store a separate authenticated-encrypted copy for authorized reviewers to reveal on demand. Never include PINs in list payloads or reviewer history. Existing hash-only PINs cannot be decrypted.

**Why:** The user asked that OTP failure or delay not block submission or reviewer access, and that email verification only unlock applicant status after submission. The user also explicitly asked authorized reviewers to see PIN values and evidence even before email verification; encrypted storage preserves that need without exposing PINs in ordinary list/history responses. Older PIN hashes are one-way and cannot be reconstructed.

**How to apply:** Keep `/dashboard` public and shared across authentication states. After collecting contact details, category selection should create/save the guest draft and open category entry/PIN directly. Keep the contact step skipped within the active session, but always provide an explicit path to the portal home and return public guest-page reloads to home. In the final applicant preview, show the admin-configured category title, category-entry labels, workflow titles, and field labels; keep configured category-entry fields visible even when unanswered. Redirect applicant-only legacy routes to `/dashboard` without deleting their database records; keep admin review paths and role checks intact. Save each guest step and private evidence server-side. Submit with the mandatory PIN and applicant declaration without requiring email verification; open the OTP dialog on the submit action, send the code once submission succeeds, and link only after a verified primary email matching the contact email. Until linked, hide submitted status details from the applicant and keep the full record visible to authorized reviewers. Protect resume access with the private tracking key, preserve prior values for reviewer comparison, and keep internal reviewer notes private. Store PIN hashes plus an independently authenticated-encrypted copy for role-guarded, explicit reveal; never expose PINs in reviewer list/history responses. Authorized admins and sub-admins may permanently clear only the applicant records they select from the review list; explain the deletion and remove associated private evidence. Do not add category, phone, or evidence fields to the first contact screen.
