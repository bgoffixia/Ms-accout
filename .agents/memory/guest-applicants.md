---
name: Guest applicant path
description: Product requirement for pre-applications without an applicant account.
---

Applicants may submit initial enrollment details without creating a Clerk account. Staff must collect the required identity files and verification video before a full verification review; do not remove those evidence requirements to enable guest access.

**Why:** The user chose guest applications and specified that applicants should submit details now and provide evidence later.

**How to apply:** Keep the guest path limited to a pre-application until staff follow-up supplies the required verification evidence.
