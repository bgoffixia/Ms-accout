name: Guest applicant path
description: Product requirements for account-free enrollment, evidence collection, and email verification.
---

Applicants can complete all enrollment details, the category PIN, and required private identity evidence/video without creating an account. The first guest screen collects only full name and email; category selection follows inside `/guest/apply`, then the saved application goes directly to category entry and PIN. Do not add a public guest dashboard or a category-confirmation page. The authenticated applicant dashboard at `/dashboard` stays private behind sign-in; retired account-based applicant URLs redirect there without deleting records or changing staff access. Clerk email verification of the same contact email is required for final submission and account linking; failure or postponement must leave the saved draft and evidence available to authorized reviewers.

Applicants may advance through enrollment steps with incomplete fields. After Clerk verification, partial applications may be submitted if the category PIN is present; missing details, evidence, or declaration must remain visible as outstanding requirements. Save every step and correction for authorized reviewers, retaining prior entries so they can compare changes. Verified applicants should see their status, verification progress, and applicant-facing reviewer follow-up, while internal notes remain private. Never include raw PINs in reviewer history or store them in plaintext.

**Why:** The user requires guest applicants to keep work through failed or postponed OTP and wants reviewers to see corrections. On October 13, 2026, the user asked to restore the pre-guest authenticated dashboard and remove the guest dashboard and category-confirmation page without changing role-based access.

**How to apply:** Keep `/dashboard` behind applicant sign-in. After guest contact details, select a category in the guest application flow, save the draft, and continue to entry/PIN without a confirmation page. Redirect applicant-only legacy routes to `/dashboard` without deleting records; keep admin review paths and role checks intact. Save each guest step and private evidence server-side before and during Clerk verification. Link only a verified primary email matching the contact email; keep drafts reviewer-visible if verification fails. Protect resume access with the private tracking key, preserve safe prior values for reviewer comparison, and keep internal reviewer notes private. Require the category PIN for submission without requiring other application fields, evidence, or declaration to be complete. Never store raw PINs. Do not add category, phone, or evidence fields to the first contact screen.
