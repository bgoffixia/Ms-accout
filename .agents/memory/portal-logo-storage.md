---
name: Portal logo storage
description: Public storage expectations for branding images versus applicant evidence.
---

Portal category logos are public branding assets and may be served to unauthenticated applicants. Identity documents, verification videos, and other applicant evidence remain private and must use their separate gated access path.

**Why:** Applicants need to see portal branding before signing in, while evidence contains sensitive personal information.

**How to apply:** Keep logo storage and serving explicitly public; do not reuse its ACL policy or routes for applicant evidence.
